## What this vertical cares about

Public sector identity work is mandate-driven: agencies move when directives, audits, and funding tell them to, and identity sits at the center of nearly every current mandate.

- **Zero trust mandates.** [PLACEHOLDER] Federal zero trust directives and their state/international equivalents put identity explicitly at the foundation of required architectures — phishing-resistant MFA, continuous verification, least privilege. Agency security teams are graded on measurable progress against these.
- **FedRAMP and compliance frameworks.** US federal buyers require FedRAMP-authorized services; states increasingly use StateRAMP; defense adds [PLACEHOLDER] IL levels; international governments have their own schemes. Compliance posture is a threshold question, not a feature.
- **Workforce modernization.** Legacy directories, smart-card/PIV-CAC integration alongside modern authenticators, decades of accumulated systems, and a workforce spanning employees, contractors, and cross-agency partners. Modernization programs are funded and multi-year.
- **Citizen-facing identity.** Digital government services need accessible, equitable, secure login for the public — CIAM with identity-proofing, at population scale, under accessibility requirements.
- Constant background pressures: tight budgets, long procurement cycles, security-clearance staffing shortages, and audit findings that force timelines.

Recruitment constraint, non-negotiable: **ethics and gift rules govern this vertical.** No comped passes, paid travel, or anything of value without compliance clearance — see the pubsec region page before any outreach.
