## The Yahoo win — Oktane as a deal accelerator

**Account team:** Mike Volpe (Regional Manager, Customer Success) and Shivani Ginde (Customer Success Executive)

**The situation:** Yahoo came into an MBR with massive, growing concerns about how AI was going to disrupt their business — specifically flagging a pressing need for AI security. The deal had a 2–3 year timeline baked in and wasn't moving.

**The play:** The team built a custom Oktane track focused on AI governance and executive engagement:
- Wednesday: ISPM Deep Dive + VIP Welcome Reception at CHICA
- Thursday: OIG Updates + Dinner at The Palm

**The result:**
- Timeline compressed from 2–3 years to **immediate budget allocation**
- Expanded deal: O4AA, OIG, OPA, and ISPM — all added at once
- **3-year early renewal**
- Direct CISO and CTO visibility gained
- Yahoo is now **speaking at Oktane** this year

> "They went from a 2-3 year timeline to immediate budget just by getting them in front of our experts to talk about Agentic AI. We completely displaced SailPoint and CyberArk."
>
> — Mike Volpe, Regional Manager, Customer Success

### What made it work

Three things drove this outcome, each replicable:

1. **AI anxiety is real urgency.** Yahoo's AI security concerns were genuine business pressure, not a sales-manufactured problem. Oktane gave them the fastest path to credible answers.
2. **Custom track, not generic invite.** The sessions were chosen for Yahoo's specific concerns (ISPM, OIG, Agentic AI) — the customer felt seen, not shepherded.
3. **Executive access compressed the cycle.** Getting Yahoo's team in front of Okta experts and executives removed months of back-and-forth scheduling that would have stretched the deal to its original 2–3 year horizon.

### How to use this story

Use the Yahoo win when:
- A customer is in a **stalled deal** or on a long timeline — this is the clearest evidence of compression
- The account has **AI security concerns** — Yahoo's trigger was identical to the AI Identity Readiness Assessment conversation
- You're pitching to an account that **competes with or resembles** Yahoo (media, digital consumer brands, complex enterprise environments with legacy IAM)
- The customer questions whether Oktane is worth the trip for a deal that isn't closing — this is the answer

Frame it as: "We built a three-session track for one customer around their specific AI governance problem. They went from a multi-year timeline to immediate budget. Here's what we could build for you."
