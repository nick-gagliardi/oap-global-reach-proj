## What this vertical cares about

Healthcare identity lives in the tension between security and speed-of-care. The priorities:

- **Patient data protection.** PHI is the most regulated and most breached data class; ransomware targets hospitals precisely because downtime is intolerable. Identity is the front door — phishing-resistant MFA and least-privilege access are top initiatives at nearly every provider.
- **Clinician workflows.** Every extra login step is measured against a clinician's patience and a patient's wait. Single sign-on across [PLACEHOLDER] EHR and clinical applications, fast badge-tap or biometric re-authentication on shared workstations, and roaming sessions between exam rooms are where identity earns or loses clinical goodwill.
- **HIPAA and audit readiness.** Access logging, minimum-necessary access, business-associate obligations, and increasingly state-level privacy laws layered on top. Payers add [PLACEHOLDER] CMS interoperability-rule identity requirements for member-facing APIs.
- **Workforce complexity.** Rotating residents, traveling nurses, contractors, and affiliated-but-not-employed physicians create constant joiner-mover-leaver churn that manual processes can't govern safely.
- Patient-facing CIAM is growing fast: patient portals, telehealth, and member apps need consumer-grade login without consumer-grade fraud exposure.
