## What this vertical cares about

Technology companies are both sophisticated buyers and builders — identity is part of their product, not just their IT stack. Priorities:

- **Developer experience.** Auth is something their engineers integrate, extend, and get paged for. API quality, SDK ergonomics, docs, extensibility, and time-to-first-login matter as much as features. A bad developer experience gets a vendor designed out.
- **CIAM as product infrastructure.** Login, signup conversion, social auth, MFA enrollment, and account security are product surfaces with metrics: conversion rate, drop-off, support-ticket volume. B2B SaaS companies add enterprise SSO/SCIM requirements as a sales blocker — "do you support our customers' IdP" gates deals.
- **Scale and reliability.** Millions of authentications, global latency, uptime SLAs. These customers read status pages and architecture docs before they read data sheets.
- **Security engineering.** Token handling, session management, machine-to-machine identity for service architectures, and increasingly securing AI agents and non-human identities — an area moving fast enough that roadmap access has real value.
- Workforce identity exists here too (fast-growing headcount, contractor churn, tool sprawl) but is usually the secondary conversation.
