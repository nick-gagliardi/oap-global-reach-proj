## What this vertical cares about

Financial services runs on trust and gets attacked for exactly that reason. Identity priorities cluster around three themes:

- **Fraud and account takeover.** Credential stuffing, social-engineering-driven ATO, and increasingly AI-assisted fraud against customer accounts. Adaptive MFA, passwordless, and risk-signal orchestration are board-level topics, not IT topics.
- **Regulatory compliance.** A dense and growing stack: [PLACEHOLDER] DORA (EU operational resilience), PSD2/open banking, SOX, PCI DSS, local banking regulators in every operating country, plus data-residency rules that shape architecture directly. Audit readiness and demonstrable access governance are constant pressure.
- **Customer IAM at scale.** Millions of retail customers across web, mobile, and partner channels, where login friction is measured in abandoned sessions and lost revenue. CIAM performance, progressive profiling, and seamless step-up authentication are competitive features, not plumbing.
- Secondary but rising: workforce identity consolidation after M&A, third-party/contractor access governance, and machine-to-machine identity for open-banking APIs.
